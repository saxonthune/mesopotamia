# System

The design answers to two goals, and both are necessary.

The first is **the game** (doc02.03): a tuning journey where the player reads the
simulation and adjusts the model until a broken herd becomes a migration, then holds
that migration against rising scarcity for a score.

The second is **the spectacle**: an aesthetically interesting lifecycle to watch
unfold, whether or not anyone is playing. The worldgen toolkit (doc02.02) and the
observability practice (doc02.01) exist as much for this as for the game — a world
worth looking at, a herd whose living and dying reads as a story. A run that scores
well but looks lifeless, or one where most of the herd dies offscreen and a remnant
marches mechanically east, fails this goal even if it satisfies the first.

Balancing serves both at once. A change is only finished when the numbers reward the
behaviour *and* the behaviour is beautiful to watch.
