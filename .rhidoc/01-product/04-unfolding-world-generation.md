# Unfolding World Generation

The world is not laid out; it is **differentiated**. Generation starts from an undifferentiated
unity — a flat grid with no parts — and proceeds by cutting that whole into nested centers, each cut
creating the positive space the next feature lives in. This is the concrete method behind the
generative sequence that doc01.03 frames philosophically: where that doc says *the world unfolds*,
this one says *how the unfolding is actualized*.

## Everything is a center; the cuts make the parts

There are no privileged feature types, only centers at different scales. The river is the major
center, carved as a least-action geodesic through the unity (doc03.01.06). Carving it does two things
at once: it places water, and it **subdivides the remaining space** into regions — some large, some
small. Those regions are not background. They are the voids, and a void is real structure: the
feature placed next is placed *into* a void, in relation to the centers that bound it. Lakes
differentiate the regions the rivers leave; the directional grain differentiates what the lakes
leave; vegetation differentiates what the grain leaves. Each layer is a finer center nested inside a
coarser one, and the coarser one is prior.

## The read-set is the method

The single rule that makes the sequence work: **each step reads the wholeness as it currently
exists** — every center already placed — not a global field computed once at the start. The read-set
is the whole method, and getting it wrong is the whole failure mode.

A feature placed by ranking a global score reads nothing about its siblings. The top entries of any
global ranking sit near each other, so the features cluster and the result reads as mass-produced —
the same blob stamped repeatedly. A feature placed by a process that reads the current state is
repelled by what is already there and drawn to the larger empty voids, so the same count of features
spreads. The desired quality is not grid-regularity, which is dead, and not raw noise, which clumps;
it is **even but irregular** — spread that never repeats, the roughness of a thing that adapted to its
neighbors rather than being dealt onto a lattice.

## Differentiation over addition, derivation over placement

Two consequences follow, and they sort every layer into one of two kinds.

**Continuous fields are derived, never authored.** Moisture, the riparian/steppe gradient, the grain
orientation, vegetation capacity — each is a function of the centers beneath it: capacity richest
near the channels that would have deposited the soil; grain bending from a global wind bias to run
along a bank as it nears water. Deriving heterogeneity from the existing field is structure-
preserving and recapitulates the real causal order of landscape formation. A layer of independent
noise overprinted on the world is an *addition* — a foreign field blind to the structure it sits on
(doc01.03 states this discipline in full).

**Discrete features are placed by a void-aware process.** A lake, a clump of grain — anything that
needs good spacing — is not derived from a field and is not ranked off one. Noise proposes
candidates; a sequential placement reads the centers already down (the rivers, and the features of
its own kind already placed) and accepts or rejects so the result breathes. This single operator —
candidates in, current-state read, spaced positions out — is the genuinely new machinery the method
needs, and lakes and grain are its first two customers. Its tools live in doc02.02.

## The order is the contract, and it is backtrack-free

Because each step reads only what precedes it, the order in which layers run is not a scheduling
detail — it *is* the structure. Standing water is fully placed before the distance-to-water field is
computed, because everything downstream reads that field; the grain is placed before vegetation,
because vegetation may read the grain; the animals navigate last, because a world must exist before
anything lives in it. No step forces the undoing of an earlier one. Reconciling two mutually
dependent layers is backtracking, and backtracking is what makes a generated world read as dead. The
generation order is a compression of causal time — geology, then hydrology, then flora, then fauna —
and the world reads as alive precisely when the order in which it is built recapitulates the order in
which such a world would form.

## The operators that actualize this

The method is a discipline about *what each step reads*; the steps themselves are operators over the
grid state, each one taking the wholeness as it stands and returning a richer wholeness. The
catalogue of those operators — what is in use, what is available, and the rule each must satisfy to
earn its place — is doc02.02.
