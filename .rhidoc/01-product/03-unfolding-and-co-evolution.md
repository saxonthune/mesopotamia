# Unfolding and Co-Evolution

The artifact is made by two generative processes of opposite character, joined at a single seam.
The **world unfolds** — built once, in a backtrack-free sequence where each layer differentiates the
layers beneath it. Then the **world co-evolves** — a dialectical loop where actors and environment
remake each other, until a quantity accumulated past a threshold becomes a change in kind. The first
process is Alexander's generative sequence; the second is Levins and Lewontin's dialectic. The seam
is the moment generation stops and the simulation starts to run.

## The world unfolds

World generation is a **generative sequence**, not a layout. Each layer reads the wholeness as it
currently stands and intensifies it, and the order is the contract: a layer may only differentiate
fields already laid down. Water is the major center — rivers carved as least-action geodesics on a
cost field (doc03.01.06). Everything finer is a *differentiation* of that center: the riparian/steppe
soil gradient reads water proximity, vegetation capacity keys to distance from water. Because each
step depends only on what precedes it, the sequence is **backtrack-free** — no step forces the
undoing of an earlier one. Reconciliation between two mutually-dependent layers is the failure mode;
it is backtracking, and backtracking is what makes a generated world read as dead.

This yields a discipline: **prefer differentiation over addition.** A layer that derives its
heterogeneity from the existing field (alluvial fertility richest near the channels that deposited
it) is structure-preserving and mirrors the real causal order of landscape formation. A layer of
independent noise laid over the world is an addition — a foreign field that ignores the structure it
sits on. Roughness still belongs in a living world, but it comes from each cell *adapting* to the
water structure, not from an uncorrelated octave overprinted on it. The generation order is a
compression of causal time: the world reads as alive when the order in which it is built
recapitulates the order in which such a world would form.

## The seam

Unfolding is one-directional by necessity — a world has to exist before anything can live in it, so
generation admits no feedback. The instant the world is finished, that constraint lifts and the
opposite process begins. This is a hard boundary in the artifact, both engineering and conceptual:
on one side, an authored, reproducible function from a single seed to a finished world; on the
other, an open dialectical loop. Generation is conservative — it preserves and extends structure.
The simulation is contradictory — it drives change. The artifact needs both, and keeping them
cleanly separated is what lets each be true to its own logic.

## The world co-evolves

The running simulation rejects the clean one-way cascade that generation depends on. Geology does
not determine water determine soil determine actors in a fixed hierarchy; **actors construct their
environment as much as it shapes them.** Niches are defined by activity. A herd grazes a bank bare,
trampling crossings into fords and fertilizing the soil it moves through — so where forage grows
follows from where the herd has been, which follows from where forage grew. Human actors irrigate,
and irrigation salts the ground that then constrains them. The environment is not a backdrop the
actors read; it is a term they co-author. The whole is prior to the parts, and the parts remake the
whole that shaped them.

This is why emergence (doc01.01) is the artifact's stance rather than a technique. The macro
patterns are not detected and fired by a controller; they are the aggregate of local rules folding
back on themselves. The dialectical loop is the emergent stance stated as a process: contradiction,
co-determination, change.

## Quantity becomes quality

Co-evolution does not drift endlessly. It runs on a **contradiction between two kinds of variable**:
the cyclical and the accumulative (doc01.02.01). A cyclical variable is a loop — debt that the
jubilee cancels, biomass that recovers, an amplitude that returns to baseline. An accumulative
variable is a ratchet — debt instruments, salinization, hoarded silver — a stock that only climbs.
For a long span the loop dominates and the system looks homeostatic: it always returns. Then the
ratchet crosses a threshold, and the loop loses the property that made it a loop. The cycle stops
closing.

That crossing is a **phase transition** — a quantitative accumulation producing a qualitative break.
The jubilee cycle, homeostatic for generations, ends not because a rule retires it but because the
accumulation of debt instruments has remade the economy into one the clean slate can no longer
reset. What was a loop becomes a one-way descent; categories that did not exist as categories —
permanent creditor and permanent debtor — crystallize. The part (a debt contract) past critical mass
reconstitutes the whole (the political economy), which redefines what a part (a household) is. This
is the artifact's central drama: not a scripted collapse, but a contradiction carried until quantity
turns into quality.

The transition is meant to be *felt*, and it is also meant to be *seen*. The qualitative break is
precisely the moment a conservation that held during the homeostatic regime fails — the cycle that
always closed stops closing. Read against the right instrument, that failure is the phase transition
made legible (doc02.03): watching the contradiction and checking the wholeness are the same act from
two sides.
