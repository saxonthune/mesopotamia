# Research Sessions

Conversation-scoped research that orients the product without belonging to any one buildable slice. (Milestone-scoped research lives with its milestone under `03-milestones`.)

Each session is a subgroup. Within a session, each deep-research pass is its own doc holding that pass's verified output verbatim — copied from the research harness, not re-synthesized. Contested claims are inventoried (in each pass's *Refuted Claims* and *Caveats*) rather than re-litigated.

## Sessions

- `01-mesopotamian-political-economy` — which economic/ecological variables cycle vs. accumulate until accumulation breaks the cycle.
