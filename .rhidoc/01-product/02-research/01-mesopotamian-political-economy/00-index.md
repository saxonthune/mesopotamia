# Mesopotamian Political Economy

A research session orienting the simulation's core thesis: across cycles, some quantities return to baseline each period (LOOP) while others accumulate one-way until the accumulation breaks the cycle (ACCUMULATOR). The goal is an inventory of which economic and ecological stocks belong on each track.

Each pass below holds the deep-research harness's verified output verbatim. Findings carry their adversarial-verification vote and sources; killed claims are kept under each pass's *Refuted Claims* so contested points are inventoried, not re-argued.

## Passes

- `01-pass1-debt-and-salinization` — debt-cancellation cycles, the agrarian-vs-commercial debt split, fixed 20%/yr interest, salinization as a managed-vs-accumulating stress, and agent-based irrigation/inequality models.
- `02-pass2-jubilee-politics-and-land-tenure` — the political triggers and legitimacy of debt jubilees (Šamaš, *kittum u mīšarum*, the army/corvée base), polity as an oikos mosaic, the Gelb/Diakonoff land-tenure debate, collapse dynamics, and population as a cycling variable.
- `03-debt-motives-trade-and-salinization` — why individuals took on debt (subsistence vs. commercial, antichretic pledge loans, the administered 20% silver / 33% barley interest), how Old Assyrian *karum* trade and commoditization worked, and the physical irrigation→salt mechanism with the Jacobsen/Adams vs. Powell debate.
- `04-household-pastoralists-husbandry-and-resources` — the rejection of the temple-state monopoly model, pastoral-settled integration (Mari's parallel district/tribal administration under Zimri-Lim, dimorphic nomadism revised toward *more* integration, the discredited Amorite-invasion theory), and Ur III institutional herding at massive scale (~320,000 sheep in one province) whose daily management escaped the archive. *Single-vote verify; the household-as-unit, forestry/mining, and luxury-goods questions yielded no surviving claims.*
- `05-silver-yields-ecology-and-karum-generalizability` — the carried-over open threads run under adversarial verify: the wheat-to-barley salinity proxy is no longer accepted as reliable (Powell's critique broadly acknowledged) though salinization itself is real; the *naruqqum* confirmed as a pooled multi-investor fund but its bearer-transferability and representativeness refuted; dowry as heritable female wealth. *Silver lifecycle, the technological ratchet, deforestation, and carrying capacity each produced no surviving claims — every silver-circulation and climate-carrying-capacity candidate was refuted.*

## Consolidated map

Distilled from the verified findings across all passes (see each pass for evidence and votes).

| Variable | Track | Note |
|----------|-------|------|
| Agrarian / personal / consumer debt | LOOP (reset by jubilee) | ~30 Clean Slates 2400–1400 BC |
| Debt-bondage | LOOP (bounded) | Hammurabi §117 caps at 3 years |
| Soil salinity, *when managed* | LOOP (conditional) | fallowing / leaching / crop-timing |
| Population | LOOP (recoverable) | decline is reconstitutable, not terminal |
| Territorial / political control | LOOP (oscillating mosaic) | not a smoothly growing blob |
| Land / wealth concentration | ACCUMULATOR | broke the jubilee mechanism itself after ~1400 BC |
| Commercial / silver debt | ACCUMULATOR | deliberately exempt from cancellation |
| Fixed interest accrual | ACCUMULATOR | 20%/yr, decoupled from productivity |
| Soil salt / silt, *unmanaged* | ACCUMULATOR (preconditioning) | lowers the threshold; proximate trigger is institutional failure |
| Distress / subsistence debt | LOOP (the canonical reset target) | harvest-timed barley loans against pledged collateral; what the *misharum* cancelled |
| Commercial / trade credit | ACCUMULATOR (exempt from reset) | year-or-less loans; *naruqqum* confirmed as a pooled multi-investor fund (pass 5) — but its *bearer-instrument transferability* is contested (asserted pass 3, refuted pass 5), so treat note-transferability as uncertain |
| Trade-good supply (metals, stone, timber) | one-way INFLOW (geographic deficit) | alluvium lacks metal/stone/timber → permanent import dependence drives the trade engine |
| Institutional flocks / animal capital | ACCUMULATOR (stored, mobile wealth) | Ur III province ~320,000 sheep; herds are weighable capital, but daily management escaped the archive — a *legibility limit*, not a stock the state fully controlled |
| Pastoral ↔ settled integration | COUPLED (not a separable sector) | Mari governed both via parallel district/tribal channels; revisionist consensus is *more* integration, not a farmer-vs-nomad dichotomy |

Institutional and "private" economies are **coupled, not separable** (Steinkeller) — do not model them as independent stocks. The institutional-vs-private land balance is a tunable, period-and-region parameter (Gelb/Diakonoff).

**On the debt motive (pass 3):** individuals borrowed mainly for *subsistence* (distress) or *commerce* — directly productive lending was scarce. Interest was *administered*, not market-set (sexagesimal 20% silver / 33% barley, stable ~2,000 years), so debt accrues on a fixed clock decoupled from the borrower's actual productivity — the structural reason the distress accumulator outruns the harvest. **On salt (pass 3):** the physical mechanism (evaporation + capillary rise under poor drainage, mitigable only by *multi-season* fallow) is undisputed soil physics; the *strong* version — that salinization was "practically uncontrollable" and caused civilizational decline — was refuted 0-3. Model salt as a *manageable preconditioning* stress, not a one-way doom.

**On structure (pass 4):** there is no single dominant institution. The temple-state monopoly model is *rejected* — temple, private, and communal holdings coexist — so the sim's actors (household, temple, palace, mobile pastoralists) are *partially autonomous units in overlapping jurisdictions*, not nested boxes under one owner. Pastoral and settled populations are **coupled, not opposed**; the Amorite-invasion-as-collapse-trigger story is discredited. And institutional herds, though enormous, were legible to the state only as *transactions and accountability*, not as *operational control* — a built-in gap between what an administration records and what it actually governs.

**On the verification trade-off (passes 4–5):** both ran a *single-vote* verify (1 skeptic, kill on 1 refute) versus pass 3's 3-vote majority. The single skeptic is markedly harsher: pass 4 confirmed 6/15, pass 5 confirmed 5/15, and whole question-clusters (silver lifecycle, technological ratchet, deforestation, carrying capacity, household-as-unit, forestry/mining, luxury goods) produced *no survivors*. Read these absences as "did not clear a strict one-skeptic gate," not as "no evidence exists." Pass 5 additionally used an **Opus synthesis merge** over Sonnet-gathered evidence (pass 4's synthesis was Sonnet) — a deliberate quality comparison of the merge step.

## Open threads

These remain starved of verifiable data. Several were *attempted* under the strict single-vote gate in passes 4–5 and produced no survivors — a future pass should retry them with the 3-vote majority and tighter sub-questions.

- **Silver lifecycle** — circulation vs. hoarding, money-supply cycling (cluster D). *Attempted pass 5: every silver-circulation candidate (incl. the dual unit-of-account/circulating-medium framing for both Babylonia and the karum) was refuted. The core money question is still unanswered by verified evidence.*
- **Technological ratchet vs. revert** — plough, seeder-plough, per-hectare yields across collapses (cluster C). *Attempted pass 5: no claims survived; the Ur III yield figures' reliability (Maekawa/Halstead) once Powell's critique is applied remains open.*
- **Biomass / deforestation specifics** — cedar, the Gilgamesh–Humbaba ecological memory. *Attempted pass 5: no survivors.*
- **Carrying capacity** — thresholds and city abandonment/resettlement tempo. *Attempted pass 5: climate-driven candidates (5.2/4.2/3.2 ka events; 2000–1500 BC demographic decline) all refuted; hard-ceiling-vs-elastic still open.*
- **Life-cycle debt** — *partially addressed (pass 5):* dowry is confirmed as heritable female wealth distinct from the husband's estate (Hammurapi §162–163). Still open whether **arrears** (tax/rent owed to palace/temple) vs. fresh consumption borrowing dominated the stock of personal debt.
- **Household as fundamental political unit** — *attempted pass 4, no survivors:* the Schloen patrimonial-household vs. Diakonoff community-based models were not adjudicated by verified evidence. New thread.
- **Forestry, quarrying & mining** — *attempted pass 4, no survivors:* how the alluvium's lack of timber/stone/ore organized royal procurement expeditions and tied into the gift/luxury circuits is unresolved. New thread.
- **Luxury-goods emergence** — *attempted pass 4, no survivors:* whether the prestige logic shifted from Early-Dynastic burial display (Royal Cemetery of Ur) to Amarna-style diplomatic gift-circulation is unresolved. New thread.

Resolved / refined this round:

- **Wheat-to-barley shift** — *resolved (pass 5):* no longer accepted as a reliable *quantitative* salinity proxy; Powell's critique (textual mistranslation + barley simply out-producing wheat) is broadly acknowledged. Salinization itself stays real and irrigation-accelerated — only the proxy is downgraded.
- **Generalizability of the *karum* model** — *refined, still open (pass 5):* the *naruqqum* is confirmed as a pooled multi-investor fund, but its bearer-instrument transferability and its representativeness (vs. an elite Assur-Anatolia exception) were refuted. How Ur III / Babylonian / Neo-Assyrian palace- and temple-administered trade differed is still unresolved.
