Demo 1 — Grazing on the river

What's going on

A river is generated procedurally: white noise is smoothed into a cost field, then a least-cost path is carved bank-to-bank, giving a meandering channel of substantial width. Distance from the water sets each tile's grass carrying capacity — lush at the banks, barren far away.

Elk spawn in packs on the left and migrate rightward under a pressure that grows over time. They steer by a boids-like blend of separation, cohesion, and the grass gradient. Grazing converts grass into energy and, after digestion, into poop that fertilizes wherever the elk has wandered — closing an ecological loop between water, grass, and herd.
