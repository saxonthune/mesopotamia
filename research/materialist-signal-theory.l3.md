# L3 — A Materialist Theory of Signal (the user's research program)

The user's self-location: **pro-Engels, the *Capital* Marx over the *German Ideology*
Marx, Levins/Lewontin dialectical-materialist science. Anti-WESTERN-Marxist, NOT
anti-Marxist.** Goal: a *theory of signal* that overcomes BOTH materialistic nihilism
(meaning is epiphenomenal noise) AND neoplatonic idealism (everything is
signifier/Idea). Dissatisfied with: New-Deal-progressive policy technocracy; Žižek-style
grifters; Monthly Review's metabolic-rift (judged overemphasized / idealistic).

## The lineage being claimed (NOT Western Marxism)
Engels (*Dialectics of Nature*, *Anti-Dühring*) → Boris Hessen / J.D. Bernal / J.B.S.
Haldane / Joseph Needham (materialist history + dialectics in natural science) →
**Levins & Lewontin, *The Dialectical Biologist*** → today. This is the "science-of-
nature-and-history" Marx, opposed to the Hegelian/critical-theory/aesthetic Western
Marxism that Perry Anderson indicts (see [[intellectual-cliques-sociology]]).

## Questions
- Q1 Does the materialist/dialectical (non-Western) Marxist strand exist as a LIVING
  research program today? Where?
- Q2 Is there a current materialist theory of *signal/information/meaning* that overcomes
  both reductive nihilism and idealism of the signifier?
- Q3 What is "the thing postmodernism/Western-Marxism answers wrongly"? (working answer:
  the ontological status of the ideal — meaning/value/signal as *real abstraction*.)

### C. Materialist theory of SIGNAL (the actual target)
- Terrence Deacon, *Incomplete Nature* (2011)  #landmark #Q2 #Q3 · teleodynamics +
  "absentials": ends/meanings are REAL but are constraints/absences, not substances.
  The single closest thing to the user's project. (Deacon also sits in biosemiotics.)
- Biosemiotics — Hoffmeyer, Kull, Emmeche, Favareau  #landmark #Q2 · life IS sign-process;
  Peirce's TRIADIC sign (vehicle/object/interpretant) decouples meaning from mind →
  meaning is a natural, material phenomenon. Directly "signal is real in nature."
- Gregory Bateson, *Steps to an Ecology of Mind*  #Q2 #Q3 · "the difference that makes a
  difference" — information as difference; materialist yet non-reductive. The OG.
- Karl Friston, Free Energy Principle  #Q2 · organism = inference/signal engine sustaining
  itself far from thermodynamic equilibrium via a Markov blanket. Mathematically grounded,
  thermodynamic material basis for "signal." (Grand/contested — handle critically.)
- Gilbert Simondon, *Individuation* + "information" as resolution of metastable tension
  #Q2 #Q3 · information neither matter nor idea by design. French but pre-postmodern
  (philosophy of technology). Yuk Hui = contemporary continuation (trendier — caveat).
- C.S. Peirce — triadic semiotics, pragmatic maxim  #foundation #Q2 #Q3 · the spine under
  biosemiotics; sign irreducible to matter OR mind. Links to pragmatism in
  [[intellectual-cliques-sociology]] (Q2 "engage reality" toolkit).
- Henri Bergson, *Creative Evolution* (1907)  #ancestor #Q3 #process-biology · attacks
  mechanism AND finalism as "two sides of the same coin" — both deny duration, both hold
  "everything has been decided." This is the EXACT structural template for Q3: finalism =
  the user's "neoplatonic idealism" (pre-given Forms/ends), mechanism = the nihilist
  reduction. élan vital = "capacity for invention," NOT a spiritual principle. Deacon's
  teleodynamics = the naturalized redemption of élan vital (ends as absential constraints,
  not a vital force). Bergson states the problem; Deacon/Shea answer it materially.
  [public domain: HathiTrust hvd.32044021581178; Gutenberg]
- Henri Bergson, *Matter and Memory* (1896)  #ancestor #Q2 · the "image" ontology — matter
  as neither pure stuff nor representation; the more direct ancestor of a THEORY OF SIGNAL
  (vs Creative Evolution = ancestor of process biology). The Bergson Deleuze mines most
  (Cinema books). Read after Creative Evolution. [public domain: archive.org]
- BRIDGE to Capital: Bergson's spatialized/quantitative time vs lived duration ≈ Marx's
  abstract labor-time vs concrete living labor. See Moishe Postone, *Time, Labor, and
  Social Domination* ("abstract time"). Ties Q3 back to value-as-real-abstraction.
- Howard Odum — systems ecology / energetics ("emergy")  #Q1 · materialist ecology as
  ENERGY FLOW, not moralized metabolic rift. The grounded alternative to Monthly Review;
  also feeds the Mesopotamia ecological-flow engine.

## Findings / annotations
- [Q1] **Yes, the strand lives — split across two homes.** Political economy: Anwar Shaikh
  (empirical classical/Marxian, "real competition") + TSSI/Kliman are the *Capital*-Marx,
  reality-tracking pole. Natural science: the Levins/Lewontin dialectical-biology torch is
  carried (mostly de-Marxified) by Developmental Systems Theory, process biology (Dupré/
  Nicholson), and niche construction. NOT in Heinrich's value-form (that's Western Marxism).
- [Q2] **Yes — biosemiotics + Deacon's teleodynamics is the live program** for a materialist
  theory of signal. Peirce's triadic sign makes meaning a natural phenomenon (decoupled from
  mind); Deacon's "absentials" make ends/meaning REAL-as-constraint, not substance; Bateson's
  "difference that makes a difference" is the seed; Friston gives a thermodynamic/inferential
  material basis; Simondon gives the metaphysics of information-as-tension-resolution.
- [Q3] **The thing postmodernism answers wrongly = the ontological status of the ideal.**
  Postmodern/Western Marxism: all is signifier/discourse (idealism of the sign — the user's
  "neoplatonic idealism"). Vulgar materialism: meaning is epiphenomenal (nihilism). The
  dialectical-materialist third answer the user wants: **signal/value/meaning are REAL
  ABSTRACTIONS — immanent constraints/patterns/differences that do causal work WITHOUT being
  substances or transcendent Ideas.** This is Engels' quantity→quality + Levins' constraint-
  ontology applied to information; Deacon's absential and Marx's value (Sohn-Rethel's real
  abstraction) are the SAME ontological category.
- [THE GAP / what's left for the user] Nobody has bundled: **(Capital-Marx value as real
  abstraction) + (Engels/Levins dialectical materialism) + (biosemiotic/teleodynamic signal).**
  The thread: *value, meaning, and signal are one category — real abstractions, materially
  efficacious, immanent not transcendent.* That synthesis overcomes nihilism (they're causal)
  AND idealism (they're not transcendent). This is the open, sparsely-occupied program.
